# Research Context (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE DATA)

> All data in `employee_survey_comments.csv` is synthetic, generated for testing. Northwind Logistics is a fictional company; no real people or responses.

## Setting
Northwind Logistics is a fictional mid-size (~1,800 employees) third-party logistics company with warehouses, a transportation fleet, and a corporate HQ. In the past year it underwent a restructure with layoffs (March), a new insurance plan, a new TMS rollout, and shifting return-to-office/hybrid policies.

## Research question
How do employees experience working at Northwind Logistics, and what drives (or erodes) their engagement?

## Sub-questions
- What do employees value about their work and workplace?
- What frustrations and barriers do they describe?
- How do experiences differ across frontline vs. office roles, departments, and tenure?

## Data
Annual engagement survey, 50 respondents, three open-ended questions:
1. What do you like most about working here?
2. What should we improve?
3. Any other comments?

Columns: respondent_id, department, tenure_band, question, comment.

## Analytic approach
First-round open coding (grounded theory, Charmaz style): no a priori codes. Stay close to the data, use gerund codes and in vivo codes where possible, write memos, and compare incidents constantly.
